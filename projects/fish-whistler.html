Fish Whistler

v1.0.0

What is Fish Whistler?

My most recent project still in development where I am learning how to create HD Terrains, Endless Oceans with Underwater Environments, Realistic Lighting, Volumetric Clouds, and Physical Skies.

I started developing Fish Whistler in July of 2026 and am still working on it. I'm developing this game using Unity High Definition Render Pipeline, 3D modeling the environment in Blender, and programming in C#.

Features

  • Volumetric Clouds
  • Endless Ocean
  • Physical Sun
  • HD Lighting
  • Custom Terrain
  • Underwater Environment
