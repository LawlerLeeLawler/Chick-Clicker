v1.0.0

What is Lost in the Grotto and Thievery?

It's a fast-paced, low-poly first-person shooter where you play as a goblin thief that must survive endless waves of armed skeletons, scavenge for loot, and unlock powerful upgrades.

I started developing Lost in the Grotto and Thievery in April 2025 and released it in May 2026. I solo developed this game using C# in Visual Studio Code, 3D modeled the characters and made poseable skeletons in Blender, and used Unity Game Engine.

If you wish to support the development of Lost in the Grotto and Thievery, please wishlist it on Steam.

Features

  • 100% Handwritten Code
  • 3D Modeling
  • Poseable Skeletons
  • Dialogue System
  • Cutscenes
  • Endless waves
  • First Person Shooter
  • Player driven shop
